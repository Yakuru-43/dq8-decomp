# Task 3.1: the function factory, functions up to 1 KB

**Goal.** Match unblocked functions of at most 1,024 bytes from
`build/queue.tsv`, one at a time, with `.claude/skills/match-function/SKILL.md`.
The pilot (`notes/pilot-review.md`) showed Qwen matches about half of the
functions up to 256 bytes and a third up to 1 KB, and none above; larger
functions wait for Claude.

**Which row.** The first row with `state` = `todo`, `blocker` = `none` and
`size` ≤ 1024. Leave every other row alone (don't claim it, don't change it).

**Each function.** The skill's loop, with its limits (30 attempts or 45
minutes), its idioms (read them before the first attempt of a session), its
verify + lint rule, and its needs-claude note when stuck. Commit matches in
small batches (`src/` and their `config/` lines only) through `tools/gate.sh`;
log one line per batch in `notes/weekly/<YYYY-Www>.md` with bytes matched.

**Parallel sessions.** Up to three sessions run at once, each in its own
git worktree and branch. Each worktree's `build/queue.tsv` has only its slice
(a contiguous address range) as `todo`, so the rule above is unchanged.
Commit on your worktree's branch; Claude merges the branches into `main`.

**Check.** `tools/gate.sh --head` passes after each batch, and
`tools/readme_progress.py` shows the bytes go up. This task never ends; Claude
replaces it when the rates or the queue change.
