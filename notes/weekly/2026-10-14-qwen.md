**P21 in four attempts**, the useful one being the delay-slot store: with
`D_003E0B1C = arg4;` written after `func_00239080()` mwcc spills `arg4` into
`$s0` at entry (+4 bytes, and every callee-saved register shifts); written
before it, the store is pushed down into the call's delay slot — legal, because
the delay slot runs before the callee — and the register allocation lands right.
mwcc only ever moves instructions *into* a delay slot, never out of one, so the
slot says which side of the call the statement was on. Second finding from the
same function: the two nested zeroing loops come out `outer=$a0, inner=$a1`
only when the inner loop's counter is declared first.
