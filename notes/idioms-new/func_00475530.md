## `lw $r,0($sN)` where the original has `lw $r,K($sM)`: char*/M2C_FIELD math forced address temps, use structs
Sign: the original addresses every field as `K($s0)`/`K($s1)` off the two argument
registers and saves only `$s0`/`$s1`; mine spills 3-4 saved registers, with
`addiu $s3,$s1,0x34` materialising `&p->field` and loads/stores going through
`0($s3)`, and a `sw $zero,4($a0)`-style frame blow-up (24 extra bytes on a
240-byte function).
Fix: define a file-local `typedef struct` with the real field offsets (nested
sub-objects get their own struct so `p->sub.f = 1.0f` is a plain `swc1`-offset
store) and pass members as `void *` args; do not spell fields as
`M2C_FIELD(p, s32 *, K)` through `char *`. CW then keeps only the two
parameter registers and emits base+offset addressing.
