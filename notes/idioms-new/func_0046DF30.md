## A counter initialised before the first call: its `daddu $rN,$zero,0` fills the `jal`'s slot and keeps the `sq` spills above the call
Sign: a function whose prologue saves several `$sN` and whose first
statement is a call; one local (`count = 0`-style) is live into the loop
after the call. The original keeps the `sq` saves contiguous and puts
`daddu $sN,$zero,$zero` in the first `jal`'s delay slot; mine sinks the
`sq` of a later-defined register past the `jal` and executes the zeroing
as a plain word after the call (CLASS reorder, 2 words).
Fix: write the zeroing assignment **before** the call, not after it (MH2
Placement "A constant in the `jal`'s slot, not the next `b`'s: move the
assignment across the call"). With the delay slot taken by the constant
copy, CW leaves the spill block where the other saves are.
Example: src/BATTLE/func_0046DF30.c (`count = 0;` on the line before
`func_1340C0(buf, arg1);` flips score=2 reorder to MATCH).
