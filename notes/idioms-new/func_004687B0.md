## A dead-looking `daddu $t0,$zero,$zero` in a `jal` slot is the callee's 5th arg in $a4
Sign: a `jal func_X` whose delay slot holds `daddu $t0,$zero,$zero` (or
`addiu $t0,$zero,K`) while the other arg setups sit before the call; the store
looks dead (t0 never read). `grep -rn "jal.*func_X" asm/ -B1` at other call
sites shows `$t0` set to real values (-0x18, -0x16, ...) -> `$t0` is `$a4`:
the callee takes **five** args. Declaring the prototype with 4 params makes CW
schedule the 4th arg's `daddu $a3` into the slot and drop the t0 store: the
function comes out 1 word short (CLASS structural) and every later line
shifts by one.
Fix: prototype the callee with all 5 params (`M2C_UNK func_2D6B20(s32, s32,
s32, s32, s32)`) and pass the literal 0; check neighbours' externs first —
`src/MENU/func_00468130.c` already had the 5-param form for this callee.
Scheduling then falls out: a3 before the call, t0 in the slot.
