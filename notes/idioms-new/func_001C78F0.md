## Stored 0/1 predicate flag with one `andi 0xFF` in the branch delay slot: `(u8)` cast on an **s32** local
Sign: a bool-ish flag from a call result (`sltu $v0,$zero,$v0; xori $a0,$v0,0x1` = `!(0 < r)`)
is `andi`'d once (in the delay slot of the type-test `beq`) and the store block's `sw $a0,N($v0)`
reads the same register — no second `andi`. Drafting `u8 flag = !(0 < r);` gives **two** andis
(one at the definition, one when the u8 local reloads for the word store; 4 B too long, CLASS
structural ~10) — and a signed `s32 r` gives `slt` where the original has `sltu`.
Fix: declare the local **s32** and cast the RHS: `u32 r; s32 flag; r = f(...); flag = (u8)!(0 < r);`
— `u32 r` gives `0 < r` as `sltu $v0,$zero,$v0`; the `(u8)` cast keeps exactly one `andi 0xFF`
(scheduled into the branch delay slot) while the store, reading an s32 register, needs no
re-extension. Write the test+store as a plain `if (e->type == 3) { *(s32 *)(e->ptr + 4) = flag; }`
— CW lays the store block out of line after the `b <epi>; li $v0,1` return stub with
`b <stub>; sw` in its delay slot. Inlining the RHS into the store instead sinks the whole
computation into the if (score ~11, wrong schedule).
