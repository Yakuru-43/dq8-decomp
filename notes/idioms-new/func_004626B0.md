## Window calls take a 5th arg in `$t0`; the window buffer needs align 16 to land at `sp+0x20`
Sign: any `jal func_379760` (window show) is followed in the delay slot by
`addiu $t0,$zero,K` or `daddu $t0,$a2,$zero` — a **fifth argument in $t0** (PS2/MW
convention: args 5+ go in the t-registers). A 4-arg prototype loses that word
(everything after shifts, CLASS structural). And the 0x160-byte window local
(passed `addiu $a0,$sp,0x20` to func_3768A0/3766A0/00461680/379760 in the same
function) does NOT land at `sp+0x20` from a plain char/s8 struct (align 1 puts it
at `sp+0x18`, frame 0x17C): the original struct is 16-aligned.
Fix: declare the extern with 5 params — `M2C_UNK func_379760(void *, M2C_UNK *,
s32, s32, s32);` — and pass the 5th like a normal arg (CW schedules the `addiu $t0`
into the delay slot itself). For the buffer: give the struct a `u128` first member
(common.h defines it; mwccps2 **rejects** `__attribute__((aligned(16)))` syntax).
Pass struct addresses to `void *` params, not `M2C_UNK *` ("illegal implicit
conversion"). Also seen here: `lhu` of a main-exe global written as `*(u16 *)0x415E98`
(twin of the matched `*(s16 *)0x415E90`).
