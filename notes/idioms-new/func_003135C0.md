## Two `if`s whose first body ends `b <next test>`: sequential ifs, not `else if`
Sign: after a cold then-block (`bnel <cond>,<cold>; <delay>`, block ends
`b <back>; nop`) the original branches **back to the next condition's test**
instead of falling through to the epilogue. Writing the pair as
`if (a) {X} else if (b) {Y}` makes CW skip the second test when `a` is true:
a `bnez` that jumps over Y's inline stores, one word per store, where the
original has `beql <b>,<far>; <store in delay slot>` and Y split across that
delay slot plus a far `b <back>; <second store in delay slot>`.
Fix: write two independent `if` statements — both run when both conditions
hold. Related but distinct from the one-return-stub idiom (idioms.md
"One return stub means nested ifs"): here the join point is a *test*, not a
return. Also in the same shape: `addiu $aN,$sX,+K` passing a struct field as
a call arg is `&obj->field`, not the field's value — declare the member
`char f[K];` (or its real array type) and pass it by name; a `void *` member
adds a `lw` and shifts everything after it.
