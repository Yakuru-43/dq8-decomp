## Reloaded halfword index in an `s32` local, and the lint-safe .cpp getter form
Sign: a halfword field is stored (`sh $zero,N($s1)`) and immediately reloaded
(`lh $a0,N($s1)`) — the CSE idiom's getter is needed — but a `s16 idx;` local
makes CW normalize it with `dsll32 $a0,$v0,16; dsra32 $a0,$a0,16` before the
multiply (2 extra words, and the compare registers move to `$v0`). The
original compares and multiplies straight out of the `lh`'s register (`$a0`).
Fix: declare the local `s32 idx;` — the `lh` sign-extends once into the
32-bit register and CW drops the roundtrip and uses `$a0` directly.
Second half: a member getter defined **inside** the struct body
(`s16 GetIdx() { return fC; }`) breaks `lint_contrived.py`'s
`STRUCT_BODY` blanking (its `[^{}]*` stops at the method's first `}`), so
every `pad*` field after it is flagged `pad-local`. Declare the member and
define it outside the struct: `s16 GetIdx();` in the body, then
`inline s16 Dest::GetIdx() { return fC; }` — CW still inlines it (the CSE
is still suppressed) and the struct body has no braces, so pads pass.
