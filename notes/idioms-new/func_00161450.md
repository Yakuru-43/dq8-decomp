## `return` at the tail of a loop folds `ld $ra` into the exit `b` delay slot
Sign: a loop whose body ends with `return;` after a call (`jal DebugPrintf` etc.),
then `b <epilogue>`. Mine emits `b <epilogue + 4>; ld $ra, N($sp)` (the restore
pulled into the branch delay slot, target one word past `ld $ra`) where the
original has `b <epilogue>; nop` with `ld $ra` as the epilogue's first
instruction at the target. Everything else matches; size is identical, one word
sits in a different place (CLASS structural, score 2).
Fix: end the loop body with `break;` instead of `return;`. The loop exit
branches to the after-loop point and CodeWarrior keeps `b; nop` there.

## Compare whose loaded value lands in `$aN`: the field is also an arg of a later call
Sign: inside a loop over a struct array, the guard's load reads into an arg
register (`lw $a2, 0x4($s0)` + `slt $v1, $s5, $a2`) where mine uses `$v1`, and
the field is reloaded (`lw $v0, 0x4($s0)`) further down for another use.
Fix: the field is passed as an argument to a call later in the same body, so
CodeWarrior pre-loads it into its arg register and the guard's compare reuses
it; the reload exists because the call clobbers `$aN`. Add the field to that
callee's prototype and pass it.
