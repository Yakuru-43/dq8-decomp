## `lwu` + `or`/`and` + `dsll32/dsra32 0` + `sw` on one word: a genuine s64 intermediate for BOTH the loaded word and the mask
Sign: a read-modify-write of a u32 word (`lwu N($aN); or/and $v1,$v1,$aN;
dsll32 $v1,$v1,0; dsra32 $v1,$v1,0; sw $v1,N($aN)`) where plain u32/s32
members and `(u32)`/`(s32)` casts all fold to plain `lw`/`sw` (Qwen notes call
it the "unsigned RMW" family; `u32` or `s32` locals + casts give score ~18,
140 vs 140 bytes but every load/store word and the two sext pairs missing).
Fix: keep the member `u32`, and put BOTH the loaded value and the shift
result in **`s64` locals**: `s64 value; s64 mask; ... mask = 1 << bit;
value = obj->word; obj->word = (s32)(value | mask);` (and `(s32)(value &
~mask)`). The `s64` value forces `lwu` (u32->s64 zero-extend load) and the
`(s32)` store cast is then a real narrowing CW cannot fold: it lands as the
`dsll32/dsra32 0` pair after the `or`/`and`. `mask` must be s64 too: with
`s32`/`u32` mask CW widens it at the `|`/`&` and inserts a SECOND pair right
after the `sllv` (+16 bytes, CLASS structural ~29); declared `s64`, CW folds
the `int`->`s64` conversion because `sllv` already sign-extends. R5900
`or`/`and`/`not` are 64-bit ops: the sloppy upper half from the mask is
exactly what the trailing sext pair re-corrects.
