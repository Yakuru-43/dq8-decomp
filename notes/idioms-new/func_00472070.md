## Partial sum of a constant offset: parenthesize `base + (idx - K)` to fill a `beql` delay slot
Sign: the original puts `addiu $v0,$sX,-K` (off an `s` register holding an index) in the
delay slot of a likely branch, and the block after it uses `addu $aN,$sY,$v0` (base + that
value) at the call. Mine computes `base + idx` into `$v0` in the delay slot and applies

the parenthesized constant first (an `addiu` from the `s` register holding `idx`),
schedules that partial sum into the branch delay slot, and leaves the add for the call.
