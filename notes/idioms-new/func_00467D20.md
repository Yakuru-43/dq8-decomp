## Cold-else `> 0` test: write `r > 0`, not `0 < r`, for the direct `blezl $sX,<cold>`
Sign: an if/else where the else (cold) block sits out of line after the then-arm
and both join at one `b <epi>; addiu $v0,1`, the branch is `blezl $s0,<cold>`
with the cold arm's first arg load in its delay slot (`addiu $a0,$sp,0x40`), and
the then-arm falls through. Drafting the test as `0 < r` (idioms.md "Constant on
the left" taken too far) gives `slt $at,$zero,$s0` + `beql $at,$zero,<cold>`: two
words instead of one, everything after shifts (CLASS structural, score 48,
248 vs 244 B).
Fix: write the plain `r > 0` — CW folds it into the direct signed `blezl` to the
out-of-line else and schedules the cold call's arg load into the delay slot
itself (same family as notes/idioms-new/func_00466790.md, but the constant is 0
so the *un*-flipped spelling is the right one: `0 <= x` needs left-constant for
`bgez`, `x > 0` needs the natural order for `blezl`).
