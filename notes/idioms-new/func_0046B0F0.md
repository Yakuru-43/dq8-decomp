## 16-byte local zeroed by `sq $zero`: a u128 union + `x.q = 0`, never `= {0, 0, 0, 0}`
Sign: the prologue zeroes a 16-byte local (e.g. a `f32 v[4]` later written
member by member) with one `sq $zero, N($sp)`. Writing `f32 vals[4] = {0, 0,
0, 0};` makes `mwccps2 -O3` emit a byte-loop instead (`addiu $v0,$zero,0x10;
addiu $v1,$sp,N; sb $zero,0($v1); addiu $v0,$v0,-1; addiu $v1,$v1,1; bnez`)
— 36 B instead of 4, CLASS structural (200 vs 164 B; everything after the
init matches, the diff just shifts).
Fix: declare a union of `u128 q` over the array (the `Vec` shape from
idioms.md "Struct copies show the member types") and write `vec.q = 0;` —
CW emits the single `sq $zero`, and member reads/writes stay `vec.v[i]`.
