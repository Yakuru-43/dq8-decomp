## Stored `(call() == 0)` as `sltu; xori 1; andi 0xFF; sw` from one register: `^ 1` + `(u8)` on an `s32` local
Sign: a call result is normalized (`sltu $v0,$zero,$v0`), inverted into an arg reg
(`xori $a0,$v0,0x1`), byte-truncated (`andi $a0,$a0,0xFF`, free-standing in the later
`beq`'s delay slot), and stored **from that same register** (`sw $a0,4($v0)`) inside the
taken arm. Drafting `(u8)(r == 0)` or `(u8)((r != 0) == 0)` folds to `sltiu` on the raw
value (wrong op, and an extra `xor` move + `andi $v1` copy at the store: 84 vs 88 B,
CLASS structural); a `bool` local + `== false` gives `xor` move + `sltiu` instead of
`xori`. Declaring the local `u8` yields **two** andi (assignment cast + u8->s32 at the
store) where the original has one.
Fix: `.cpp` (inside `extern "C" { }`), declare the local `s32` (not `u8`!) and write the
cast once at the assignment: `flag = (u8)((r != 0) ^ 1);` before the `if`. The `^ 1` keeps
the bool materialized (`sltu`) then inverts it (`xori` 0x1) without folding; the single
`(u8)` gives the one `andi`, which the scheduler sinks into the branch delay slot, and the
`s32` local stores with no second conversion.
