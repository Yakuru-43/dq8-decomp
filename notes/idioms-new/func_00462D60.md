## Search loop with one exit: `break` keeps the found body cold and a `b` back to the exit stub
Sign: a `for` loop scanning a fixed-count table where the body's `if (...)` found-block
ends the function. The original places the found body *after* the loop test block, its
last store sits in the delay slot of a `b` **back** to the loop-exit stub (`b <epilogue>;
ld $ra`), and the guard `return`s branch to a second epilogue entry (bare `ld $ra` just
before the saved-register reloads). My version with `return` in the found body places
that block inline and falls through into the epilogue: one word short, and every guard
branch offset is shifted by one (`beqz` immediate 0x30 vs 0x31).
Fix: write `break;` instead of `return;` at the end of the found body. The loop-exit
stub then becomes the single shared exit, CodeWarrior cold-places the found block after
the test/increment blocks and emits the back-branch with the last store in its delay
slot. The two guard `return`s above the loop stay `return`.
