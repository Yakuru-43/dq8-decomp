## Callee result kept as `s32`, not a pointer type, so `+offset` stays per-use
Sign: original does `addiu $a0, $sN, 0x20` at **every** use of a call result,
but yours folds `result + 0x20` once into the callee-saved register
(`addiu $sN, $sN, 0x20` then `daddu $a0, $sN, $zero` copies).
Fix: declare the callee result as a plain `s32` local (and the caller extern
as returning `s32`), and write `x + 0x20` at each call site; a pointer-typed
(char*/struct*) local invites CW to strength-reduce the shared offset into
the register. Pass it to the callee with a cast when it wants a `char *`.
