## Absolute-address hardware registers as `volatile` macros, no symbols
Sign: asm reads/writes raw addresses with `lui $v0,(K >> 16)` + `lw/sw off($v0)`
where spimdisasm prints `(K & 0xFFFF)($v0)` or a negative `%lo` (e.g. `-0xAE0`) —
no `D_` symbol, no `%gp_rel`. Typical of the VU/VIF area (0x1000B000-0x1000B440,
0x1000F520/0x1000F590, 0x10002010/0x10002020) in game_* under a WaitSema/SignalSema
guard. A plain `extern s32 D_x` CSEs a reload the original does, and
`__attribute__((section(".data")))` needs a symbol_addrs entry the region lacks.
Fix: per register, `#define REG_X (*(volatile s32 *)0x1000F520)  // hw-register`
(one mark on the `volatile` line, the lint accepts it; uses elsewhere are clean).
CW folds each use to `lui + lw/sw off` itself, rematerializing the `lui` per block
exactly like the original, and volatile keeps the double read (once for `| bit`,
once for `& ~bit` of the *other* register). Pair with the delay-slot rules:
`WaitSema(unit->sema)` emits `jal WaitSema; lw $a0,0x40($a0)` (arg load in the
delay slot), and a field store written **before** the next call (`unit->m44 = 0;`
then `func_001208E0();`) lands in that call's delay slot. An empty spin
`while (REG_STAT & 0xF0) {}` emits `lw; andi; nop; nop; nop; bnez L; nop` verbatim.
