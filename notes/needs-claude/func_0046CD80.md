## What it does
Gets an object via `func_0046DDF0(arg0)`, computes two 0/1 u8 flags
(`none = arg0 <= 0`, `some = !none`, each via `sltu $zero,x / xori 1 /
andi 0xFF`) and draws three text IDs through the text routine
`func_2D38E0(obj, id, flag, -1)`: 0x1F58 and 0x1F61 with `some`, 0x1F4C
with `none`.

## What I tried
- `char *obj` → mwcc rejects `char*`→`M2C_UNK`(=s32) implicit conversion.
- `u8` locals + `s32 arg0`: `slt` not `sltu` (both compares), extra
  `andi $a2,$s1,0xFF` at the third call's arg setup.
- `u32 arg0`: fixes the first `sltu`; second compare still `slt` (u8
  promotes signed).
- `(u8)!(0 < x)` into **u32** locals (pattern from the matched
  src/SLES_539.74/func_001C78F0.c): fixes both `sltu`s AND the call-site
  `andi` — body then matches fully.
- `obj` type `M2C_UNK`(s32) → `u32`: score 10 → 7 (`some`→`$s1`,
  `none`→`$s2` now correct).
- Declaration-order swaps of `none`/`some`/`obj`: no effect on the s0/s1
  split (unlike the Saved-registers idiom cases).
- MH2 catalogue entry tried: **"Saved registers in another order"**
  (index lines "A parameter in another saved register: a typed or
  same-type local copy of it, or dropping one" and "a local's integer
  type moves it in the numbering" — the type change helped, the copy
  tricks untried).
- `tools/permute.py func_0046CD80 --bin SHOP` unusable here: fails with
  `FileNotFoundError: 'cpp'` (no system cpp on this host).
