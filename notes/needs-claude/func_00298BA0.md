## What it does
Linear scan of a byte-keyed array: `count = *(s32 *)(a0 + 0x2C)`, elements at
`*(u8 **)(a0 + 0x10)` with stride 4, compare the element's first byte with `a1`
(`beq $a1, $v1`, full-word, no andi), return the byte pointer or 0.
The base is **reloaded every iteration** (`lw $v1, 0x10($a0)` sits in the `bnel` delay
slot); the loop is bottom-tested: entry `b T; i=0`, body at T-0x18, `slt; bnel` at the
bottom, `v0=0` after it, `jr ra`.

    s32 count = M2C_FIELD(arg0, s32 *, 0x2C);
