# needs-claude: func_00460EE0 (BATTLE, 160 B)

Best attempt: `CLASS structural score=38 (35 words differ, 140 vs 152 bytes)`
— but that number is misleading: the only real difference is that CodeWarrior
refuses to give the local `r` (the `func_00460FF0` result) a saved register.
Everything else is a pure permutation of `$sN` numbers that resolves as soon
as `r` gets `$s0` (then `i→$s1`, `count→$s2`, `entries→$s3` and the frame
becomes 0x50). The three missing words are `sq $s0,0($sp)` (in the guard's
delay slot), `daddu $s0,$v0,$zero` (in the `jal func_00460FF0` delay slot)
and `lq $s0` in the epilogue.
