## Blocker
Cold-arm register allocation/CSE: the ROM caches `reader->stream` in `$a1`
across the whole cold block — `st->left` (8(a1)), `st->limit` (18(a1)), the
first `*cur = *p` store's cur load `lw v0,0(a1)`, and the cur++ arm's
`lw v1,0(a1)`/`sw 0(a1)` all use the cached $a1 (only the `*cur == 10`
read-back reloads `lw a1,0x1C(s3)` then `lw v0,0(a1)`). My draft reloads
`reader->stream` at every access (a0 not a1, extra `lw`s, and the merge
`b` offset shifts because the blocks grow). Hoisting `Stream *st` into a
local at the top of the cold arm (draft 2) makes CW fold the left/limit
loads differently and the cur++ arm: 292 B, score 27 — worse. Suspect the
ROM's source used an inline helper or a form where CW's own CSE keeps $a1
live (note the fast arm also keeps its cur in a reused v0/v1 pattern).
Minor: my `b` back-edge at +0E8 vs the ROM's forward `b +3` follows from the
same layout. The `jal func_000000` stars at +02C/+0F0/+100 are unresolved
relocations (func_00130FD0/138828), ignore.
