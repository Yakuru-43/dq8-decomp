## What it does

Calls `func_00176500(i)` for every `i` in 0..12 except `i == 6`. The
increment is duplicated on both paths of the guard (`addiu $s0, $s0, 1` as
the fallthrough of the guard and again in the delay slot of the back branch
after the call), loop test at the bottom.

differs:

```
orig:  slti $at, $s0, 0xD  /  bnez $at,  .L00307550   (2A01000D)
```

wrote; the original got the `$at` macro form.
