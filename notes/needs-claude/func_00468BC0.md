## What it does
Triple loop, leaf, returns 1. `count = D_003E17A8` (gp-relative `.sdata`, loaded
once, only feeds the limit `count + 1` in `addiu $a0,$v1,1`). For
`i = 0..count` (CW while-layout: entry `b <test>`, `slt`/`bnel` bottom), each
iteration walks a 5x5 table of 8-byte cells at the absolute `D_0047FE60`
(row stride 40 = `(j<<2+j)<<3`, cell stride `k<<3`) and:
- `if (j == 2 && k == 2)` stores 1 at cell+4 (two plain `bne` with nop slots);
- `if (D_0047FF30[i] == cell->unk0)` stores 1 at cell+4
  (`beql $a1,$v1,+4` with the `sw $a3,4($a2)` in its delay slot).
`&D_0047FF30[i]` is computed once per i (`addu $t3,$t4,$v1`, the `sll $v1,$v0,2`
sits in the `bnel` delay slot) but the value is re-`lw`d per k iteration.
j/k loops are bottom-tested do-whiles (`addiu; slti $v1,r,5; bnez`), no entry
guard — the do-while form matched (the `for` form gains a `b`,
notes/idioms-new/func_0046B6B0.md).
