arm; the `andi size,0xF` sits in its delay slot (runs always). Negative
arm: `beqz rem → join; nop; addiu rem,-0x10` (i.e. C-style negative
remainder — dead except for the two zero tests). Then the join tests
`rem == 0` with **beql** + `sra q,size,4` in its delay slot, and both arms
re-test the sign: `r!=0`: `bgez → q+1`, negative falls `addiu size,15;
sra q`; `r==0` (at 0x654): `bgez → q`, negative falls `addiu size,15; sra q`.
So four arms: size>=0 → `size>>4` (+1 if rem); size<0 → `(size+15)>>4` (+1
if rem). All four are reachable and distinct — not plain `/16` or `%16`
(both are branchless here), so it is hand-written.
