## What it does
Scans the 32-entry table of 0x590-byte entries based at `&D_00421AD0`
obtained through the identity getter `func_002975E0`. Keeps the entry
minimising `func_0012D4C0(key - (byte@0x10<<8 | byte@0x12))` with
`key = (kind & 0xFF) << 8 | (sel & 0xFF)`, where:
- the half at 0x10 being 0/1 means "free slot": only valid for `kind < 2`;
  rejected for `kind >= 2`;
- `func_00201760(entry, 0) > 0` and `func_0035E570(entry) == 0` required;
- when `mode != 0`, entries with f10==kind AND f12==sel are skipped.
Returns the best entry (0 if none), after
`func_00300CB0(0 < best, &D_003CBEA0, 0xCCD)` (assert-like).
