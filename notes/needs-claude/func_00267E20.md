## What it is
A PRNG: 12 LCG steps `*seed = *seed * 0x5D588B65 + 1` (`s32 *seed`, `lw r,0($a0);
mult|mult1 v,r,$t5; addiu v,1; sw v,0($a0)` alternating mult/mult1), then 12 terms
`(2^-32 constant) * (f32)(u32)Vk`, `k = 1..12`, accumulated with the FPU accumulator:
`mula.s $f16,$f15; madda.s $f18,$f17; ... madda.s $f12,$f11; madd.s $f1,$f14,$f13`
(constant in the even reg, converted value in the odd). The `(f32)(u32)` cast is
CodeWarrior's unsigned-convert dance: `bltzl r,<neg>; srl t,r,1 | mtc1 r,$fN; b
<join>; cvt.s.w | andi t,r,1; or t,t,r; mtc1; nop; cvt.s.w; add.s fN,fN,fN`
(idioms.md "bltzl $vN; srl then andi 1; or..." — write `(f32)(u32)x`, no if).
Constants: `lui 0x2F80` = 0x2F800000 = **2^-32** (not 0.5!), folded fine from
`1.0f / 4294967296.0f`; the tail loads `lui 0x40C0` = 6.0f → `sub.s $f0,$f1,$f0`,
i.e. `return sum - 6.0f;` (6.0f's `lui` sits early, at +0x154, so 6.0f may be a named
local used before... or just scheduling luck). Prologue saves f20–f23 with `swc1`
at 0/4/8/0xC($sp), frame 0x10, `addiu $sp,$sp,0x10` in the `jr $ra` delay slot.

## Structure of the original
All 12 LCG blocks come FIRST (0x267E40–0x267F04, ending with the 13th load
`lw $a0,0($a0)` of the last value), THEN the 12 conversion blocks (each preceded by
its own `lui 0x2F80; mtc1` of the constant), then the madda chain.
Conversion of Vk reads the register that loaded Vk as the **input of LCG step k+1**
($a1=V0..$t4=V1, step order: a1,t4,t3,t2,t1,t0,a3,a2,a1,v1,v0→t6,t6→t5); V12 is
reloaded into $a0. So the C reads `*seed` after every store — CW never keeps the
computed value across the store — and the per-term load was CSE'd with the next
step's load (13 loads, not 24).
