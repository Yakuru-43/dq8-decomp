## Diff summary (mine | original)

Every region matches instruction-for-instruction once the register names are
shifted, but CodeWarrior allocated **one different register set globally**:

- original: `self→s5, mode→s4, obj→s3, list→s2, i→s1, entry→s6, count→s7, j→s0`,
  saves 8 regs (ra + s0..s7), stack 0x90.
- mine: `self→s0 (shared with j), mode→s5, obj→s4, list→s3, i→s2, entry→s1,
  count→s6`, saves 7 regs, stack 0x80 → 20 bytes short, and every `daddu/lw/sw`
  line differs by the register number (that is what the 182 differing words are).

Key first-difference region: prologue, mine
`sq s6..s0; daddu s0,$a0; beqz s0; daddu s5,$a1; ...` vs original
`sq s7..s4; daddu s5,$a0; sq s3; daddu s4,$a1; sq s2,s1; beqz s5 (delay sq s0)`.
