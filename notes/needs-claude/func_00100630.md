## What it is

Twin of `func_00100500` (read `notes/needs-claude/func_00100500.md` first —
the stack-slot spill wall and everything deducible there applies here too).

`char *func_00100630(objects, construct, destroy, size, count)`:

- `if (objects == 0) return objects;` (`beqz a0 → 710`, shared exit
  `daddu $v0,$s2` in its delay slot; s2 is still `objects` on that path).
- header stores: `sw $s4(a3) → 0(s2)`, `sw $s3(t0) → 4(s2)` —
  `*(s32 *)objects = size; *(s32 *)(objects + 4) = count;`.
- `p0 = objects + 0x10` is formed as the **delay slot** of
  `beqz $s5(construct), .L00100710` (`addiu $s2,$s2,0x10` — executes on
  both paths after that branch), i.e. `if (construct == 0) return p0;`.
- loop A from p0: `construct(p, 1)` count times, p advancing by size
  (register version: s1 moves, s3=t0 bound, s4=size step, s5=callee).
- teardown identical to 500: guard `sltu i<count_copy` (copy at `0($fp)`,
  fp = sp+0xA8), `lw destroy(0($s6), s6=sp+0xAC)`, `lw size(0($s7),
  s7=sp+0xA4)` + `mult v1,size,i`, `lw v0,0xA0($sp)` (p0 spill, saved at
  0x698 via `sw $s2,0xA0($sp)`), `beqz i` + delay `addu s1,p0,prod`,
  do-while `subu p,size; jalr f2(p,-1);` counter at `0($s0)`, s0=sp+0xB0.
- all exits `b .L00100710`, delay `daddu $v0,$s2` → **returns p0**.
- frame -0xC0; 9 saved (ra, s0-s7, fp).
