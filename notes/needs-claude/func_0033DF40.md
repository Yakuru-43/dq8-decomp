## Layout (solved — the hard part)

* `struct ShipMsg` is **0x90 bytes**, not 0x88: `func_00131188(&msg, 0,
  0x88)` zeroes only the 0x88 prefix, but msg2@sp+0xC0 and msg@sp+0x150 are
  0x90 apart. Draft's 0x88 struct lost 16 B of frame (0x1E0 vs 0x1F0).
* The `&out` passed to `func_00161E80` (blocks 3+4) is `sp+0x1EC`: the top 4
  bytes of a **16-byte local block** at sp+0x1E0. A plain `s32 out;` lands at
  the frame top minus 4 but leaves no room; modelled as
  `struct OutArg { u8 pad[0xC]; s32 out; };` declared as the **first** local
  (declaration order controls mwcc's stack order here; declared 7th it landed
  between msg2 and msg). Frame `addiu sp,-0x1F0` + boxes @0x80/0x90/0xA0,
  vec @0xB0, msg2 @0xC0, msg @0x150 then all match.
* Guard 1 needs `D_004536B4 = 0;` written **before** the `func_001629F0`
  call (P21 idiom: gp store in the jal delay slot); the draft missed it.
