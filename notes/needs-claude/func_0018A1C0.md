## The remaining diff

The original copy is a software-pipelined block copy: `lui $v1,%hi` reused
per load, FP regs rotate `f5,f4,f3,f2,f1,f2,f0,f1,f0` two-deep

`D_003C2FD8`/`D_003C2FDC` hoisted early into the free arg regs `$a1`/`$a0`
and `lui $a2,0x3F80` hoisted above the whole sequence; `sb zero,0x6964`
sits at +0x20 between the first `lui` and the first `lwc1`.

My field-by-field version instead keeps `f0` only, one separate page
