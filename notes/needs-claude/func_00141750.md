# func_00141750 (224 B, 0x141750..0x141830)

Small wrapper in the `game_140B00` / packet-builder family (see
`func_001414D0.md`). Copies two groups of four `u32` quads — one group from
`u32 *src` (arg2, `a2`) into stack slots `0x40..0x4C`, one from `u32 *hot`
(the hidden 5th argument in `$t0`, `param_reg(8)`, like
`func_00141020`'s own signature) into `0x30..0x3C` — scales every slot
in place by `<< 4`, then tail-enters `func_00141020(obj, arg1, hi, arg3, lo)`
with `a2 = sp+0x40` and **`$t0 = sp+0x30`** (both arrays are pointers;
the callee keeps them in `$s4`/`$s3` and reads `0/4/8/0xC` of each).

The only caller is `func_001DEF18` region in `game_1DE4B0.s` (jal at
0x1DEF18 with `addiu $t0,$sp,0x130` in the delay slot).

## Frame and register shape (from the asm)
- `addiu $sp,-0x50`; `sd $ra,0x20`; `sq $s1,0x10`; `sq $s0,0x0`; the two
  arrays live at `0x30..0x3F` (hot) and `0x40..0x4F` (src).
- Prologue precomputes **six** slot pointers as live registers:
  `t4=sp+0x44, t3=sp+0x48, t2=sp+0x4C, t1=sp+0x34, v1=sp+0x38, v0=sp+0x3C`;
  slot `0x40` and `0x30` are stored through `$sp` immediates instead.
- Each element except `hot[3]`: raw `lw` from source → raw `sw` to the slot
  → later `lw` reload → `sll 4` → scaled `sw`. `hot[3]` skips the raw store
  (`lw s1,0xC(t0); sll t4,s1,4; sw t4,0(v0)`). `hot[0]`'s scaled store sits
  in the `jal` delay slot. `$s0` holds `src[1]<<4` across the schedule,
  `$s1` is reused for `hot[2]`/`hot[3]` raw loads.

## Best attempt (score 39, 224 vs 224 bytes, identical epilogue)
`notes/needs-claude/func_00141750_best.c` — two-phase unrolled C: eight raw
copies `hi[i] = src[i]; lo[i] = hot[i];`, then eight `*= 16`, then the call
with both array pointers. try_match with `-- -O3` reproduces the exact
**instruction sequence position by position**; only register coloring
differs (mine rotates `$v0/$t6/$t7` for the raw loads where the original
rotates `$t5/$t6`, and the six-pointer prologue comes out one register
higher: `$t5,$t4,...,$v1` vs original `$t4,$t3,...,$v0`). CW CSEs some
store/reload pairs (e.g. `hi[0]`: mine emits `lw; sll; sw` straight, no raw
store) where the original keeps them — register-pressure dependent, which
is why the coloring is off.
