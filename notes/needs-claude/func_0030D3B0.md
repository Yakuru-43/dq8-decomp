## Why stuck

1. **$a1 persistence across `jal func_0030D4C0`.** On both paths into the
   pmf call, $a1 = &e1 (set 20D3EC, as the D180 arg) or &e2 (20D430), and
   `jal func_0030D4C0` (20D3F0/20D434) does NOT clobber it — its asm
   (same file, +0x110) only uses a0/v0/v1. A standalone compile treats
   every call as clobbering a1, so CW keeps `e` in $s2 (mine) and
   rematerializes; the frame grows to 0x50 with a third saved register.
   The original build had link-time/interprocedural register-usage info.
   No `// flags:` precedent exists for that in src/ (only divbyzerocheck).
2. **s0/s1 roles swapped.** ROM: count→$s1 (prologue `daddu s1,a1`
   first), obj→$s0 (first guard's delay slot). Mine with identical first
   uses: count→$s0, obj→$s1. Probably follows from (1) — different saved
   set, different assignment order (cf. "Saved registers follow
   declaration order", SKILL.md).
3. **unaligned load.** `ldr/ldl $a1, 0x170(s0)` — mine with
   `*(s64 *)((char *)obj + 0x170)` and with `struct CmdBuf { s64 cmd
   __attribute__((aligned(1))); }` both give aligned `ld $a1, 0x170`. The
   same problem hit func_0030CE80 (its note). What CW spelling yields
   ldl/ldr here is unknown.
4. **u8 tail.** ROM `sltu $v0,$zero,$v0; xori 1; andi 0xFF`; mine
   (u8 return, `== 0` guard) gives `xor $v0,$v0,$zero; sltiu 1` — no
   andi, inverted form. The guard spelling for this family (cf. func_0030D4C0
   note: "return-1 materialised before the tests") needs a fresh look.
5. CW also recomputes `obj+0x168`/`obj+0x160` per use in the ROM; mine
   CSEs them into $s2 (`addiu $s2, $s1, 0x168`).
