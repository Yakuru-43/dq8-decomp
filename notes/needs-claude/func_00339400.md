the operand of the div/mod block** (my first draft wrongly used `total-count`
across the call and gained a $s2 spill; using the call result dropped it).

(CW's signed %16 expansion `bgez/andi, beqz/addiu-0x10` then the beql-delay

`sw s1, 0xC(...)`, return 1. Frame 0x30, saves `sd $ra,0x20; sq $s1,0x10; sq
