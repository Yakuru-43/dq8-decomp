## The blocker: local slot layout
Original: `buf` at 0x60..0x6F (16-aligned), `val` at 0x5C. Both drafts place
`val` at 0x6E (CW packs the u16 at frame top, 2-aligned) and `buf` at 0x50
(16-aligned *below* val) — the exact bug the 5E30 note hit (their val landed
0x6E instead of 0x6C). Tried: `u16 val` before / after `char buf[16]`; both
give 0x6E/0x50. `func_00130FD0(buf, &seed, count)` (param-address form) not
yet tried. Suspect the original has a second 2-byte local occupying
0x5E..0x5F (nothing reads it), or val is a member of a struct local whose
base aligns buf to 0x60, or buf is not char[16] (e.g. char[12] like 5E30
with two locals below). Fixing this shifts the whole function into place:
draft 1 already matches size (300 B) and differs only in the two `addiu
$sp`/`sh`/`lhu` slots, the loop-entry ordering, and the `lb a0`-duplication
at +0xB8.
