## What it does
GS packet writer: `writer` is the `GSWriter *` of the matched `func_0013F240`/`func_0013EFF0`
family. Opens with `func_0013F240(writer, 4, 0x43, 0, 2)` (5th arg in $t0), sets up three
16-byte stack quads (`0xA0` pkt0, `0xB0` pkt1, `0xC0` pkt2) and a float pair at `0xD8/0xDC`
(union of `f32 f[2]` / `s32 w[2]`, copied from `D_003DFCA8/D_003DFCAC`), then loops
`i = 0..16` pushing 4 quads per pass via `func_0013EFF0(writer, pkt)`; closes with
`func_0013F0E0(writer)`.

1. **The three 16-byte zero loops.** Original has CW's inline memset expansion:
   `addiu $vN,$sp,0xC0; beqz $vN,+0x20; addiu $v0,16; loop sb $zero; addiu -1; addiu +1;
   nop nop; bnez`. This is the sign in notes/idioms-new/func_0046B1A0.md ("compiles the
   init to a byte-by-byte memset loop") — a `= {0,0,0,0}` initializer of a `f32/s32[4]`.

   `s32 pkt2[4] = {0,0,0,0};` is a **compile error** in mwccps2 (C99 off). The memsets
   sit *between* statements (0xC0 loop after the two opening calls, 0xB0/0xA0 after the
   145440 store), so either try nested `{ }` scopes with declarations at their top, or
   find the memset builtin spelling (exact prototype? `unsigned long` size? a
   `// flags:` that enables the intrinsic). This is the thing to crack first.
