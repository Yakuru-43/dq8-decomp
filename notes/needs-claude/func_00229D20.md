## What it does

Normalizes/midpoints two vec4s via the VU0. Loads `vf10=(a0)`, `vf11=(a1)`
with `lqc2`, computes `d = vf10 - vf11`, `d*d`, a QMFC2/QMTc2 round trip
through `mfc1 f12/f13` + `qmtc2`, a `vrsqrt` reciprocal-norm refinement
(`vsubq`/`vmul` Newton step on `vf20`), then
`vf10 -= d*vf13x; vf11 += d*vf14x` and `sqc2` stores back. Looks like a
spring/constraint correction between two world points.

## Why no attempt

The body is VU0: `lqc2/sqc2`, `vsub/vadd/vmul/vmulx/vadday/vmaddz`,
`vrsqrt`+`vwaitq`/`vsubq`, `mfc1`/`qmtc2` moves between GPR and VU0 macro
regs. mwccps2 3.0.1b151 never emits VU0 from C; only inline `asm {}` can,
and that is exactly what the existing asmfunc stub already is (verified
byte-exact, but git-ignored and outside the progress metric). No C draft was
attempted; no try_match run.
