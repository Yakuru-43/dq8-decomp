## The walls

1. **`mult`/`mult1` with a constant multiplicand.** Every plausible draft
   (`1 * b` s32, `(s64)1 * b`, `one * b` with `s32 one = 1`) constant-folds
   to a copy + `dsll32/dsra32` sign-extends — CW never emits the multiply.
   The original keeps `mult` (prodA) and `mult1` (prodB) — on R5900 `mult`
   is 64x64→128 and `mult1` 32x32→64, so prodA's operands look s64 to CW and
   prodB's fit 32 bits, with `$a0`/`$v0` registers (values 1 and the byte).
   A multiplicand CW knows is 1 should fold ⇒ suspect s64 types (a `s64
   one`? a macro?) or CW's s64 mul path skipping the fold. **Untested lead:**
   declare `s64 func_002970D0(s32, s32);`-style s64 returns and s64
   `roll`/products — the asm compares `slt $at,$s1,$v0` with **no**
   sign-extend of `$v0`, i.e. CW believed the rand result was already a
   valid 64-bit value (plausible: rand's tail is `addu $v0,$s1,$v0`, a
   32-bit op ⇒ sign-extended on EE, so s64 is *semantically* fine).
2. **Extra callee-saved reg** (mine `$s3` @ 0x30, frame 0x50): CW hoisted
