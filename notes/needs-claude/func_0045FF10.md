## Why it is stuck
1. **The 0xA0 local.** Original: before the loop
   `addiu $v0,$sp,0xB8; lw $v0,0($v0); sq $v0,0xA0($sp)` (stores `$v0:$v1`,
   `$v1` = 0 from the finished copy loop), and **every loop iteration**
   reloads it with `lq $v0,0xA0($sp)` for `slt $at,$s4,$v0; beqz exit`
   (`filled < bound`), then `slti $at,$s3,4; beqz exit`. `u128` gives a
   bool compare (`slt; xori 1; bnez`, +24 B, score 155); plain `s64` gives
   `dsll32/dsra32` sign-extension of the *other* operand and a register
   copy instead of the lq (score 156). The 16-byte slot that is `sq`-stored
   once and `lq`-read per iteration, only its low 64 bits ever used, is
   unresolved — a type or spelling I did not find.
2. **Register swap.** Original: row→`$s2`, slot→`$s3`, filled→`$s4`; mine
   allocates row→`$s4`, filled→`$s2` (init order `slot=0; row=arg1;
   filled=0` matches the original schedule but not the allocation).
3. **Copy loop.** Original sets `addiu $v1,$zero,0x33` **immediately after**
   `addiu $sp,$sp,-0x180` (counter in `$v1`, before `sd $ra`); mine puts the
   counter in `$a1` and the dst pointer in `$v1` and schedules the `li` at
   +0x24. Body order lw / addiu −1 / sw / +4 / +4 / `bgtz`.
4. Fill-loop entry: original shares one test block
   (`slti $v0,$s4,4; bnel $v0,$zero,<body>; daddu $a0,$s5` — the `win` arg is
   hoisted into the **delay slot**, body sits *before* the test). Bare
   `while (filled < 4)` and `if (filled < 4) do {} while (...)` both came out
   8–24 B wrong here (idioms.md "top guard + do-while" tried).
