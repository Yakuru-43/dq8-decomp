- What it does: copies two 96-byte records (`{s32; s32; Pair item[11];}`) from
  `a1`/`a2` into `obj+0x10` / `obj+0x70` (each copy = 2 scalar words + one

  `obj->p2 = &obj->rec2;`.

is loaded after call 1 in both, i.e. inline in the second call's arg list.
