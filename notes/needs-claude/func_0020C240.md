## Remaining diff (both attempts)

The logic compiles right; two scheduling/allocation issues remain.

1. **`p + 0x14` CSE.** The original loads `lw $v1, 0x14($s1)` for the
   compare and stores `sw $s0, 0x14($s1)` after the call, both with the
   offset folded into the instruction. CodeWarrior instead materializes
   `addiu $s1, $p, 0x14` and uses `lw $v1, 0($s1)` / `sw $s0, 0($s1)`,
   which makes it save a third callee register (sp 0x40 vs 0x30) and
   shifts the epilogue. Same CSE-across-call disease as func_0020C180,
   where a `void *arg0` + `char *obj` copy defeated it; here `p` is
   already a plain `char *` local and neither `p`-vs-copy nor
   separate-variable variants changed anything.
2. **bne vs bnel.** Original: `bnel $s0, $v1, .L4` with
   `lw $a0, 0x4($s1)` in the delay slot, and `b .L5; ld $ra` when the
   values are equal. Mine: plain `bne` (operands swapped) with
   `addiu $s1, $s2, 0x14` stolen into the delay slot, and the `p[4]`
   load after the branch. Fixing (1) may fix (2) by freeing the
   scheduler — issue (1) is the root cause.
