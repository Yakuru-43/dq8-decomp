## What it does

Identical shape to `func_00104060` with the `D_003DD6C0 / D_003DD6CC /
D_003DD6D8` vtable trio and a 16-bit pin:

- `if ((s16)kind != 0)` (copy in `$s0`, `dsll32/dsra32` from `$s0`, `bnez`,
  `daddu s1,a0` in the delay slot): the else block at `+0x18C` stores
  `*(void **)o = (char *)o + 0x18` and `*(void **)((char *)o + 0x18) =
  &D_003DD6D8`, then branches back to the common head.
- common head: `*(void **)((char *)o + 0x10) = &D_003DD6C0;`
  `p = *(void **)o; *(void **)p = &D_003DD6CC;`
  `*(s32 *)((char *)p + 0x48) = (char *)o + 0x18 - (char *)p;` (plain `subu`),
  `sd zero` at +8, reload `p = *(void **)o` into `$s2`, `func_00102B30(p,
  mode)` (`daddu a1,a2` scheduled early at `+0x3C`), then `sw zero, 0x40(p)`,
  `sh 0x20, 0x44(p)` in the delay slot of the `b` to the epilogue, and
  `daddu v0, s1` (return o) before it.
- The three loads of `*(void **)o` are separate `lw` (`v1, v1, s2`); my C
  reproduces that (stores through typed pointers force the reloads).

## The blocker: register plan (same as func_00104060)

Original saves 4 slots (`sq s0@0, s1@0x10, s2@0x20, sd ra@0x30`, frame 0x40)
and copies `kind` into `$s0` at `+0x14`, truncating **from `$s0`**; `kind` is
never read again anywhere in the function (checked against the full
disassembly) — a dead save. My build is 160 B, frame 0x30, `o→s0`,
`buf→s1`, test folded onto `$a1` (`dsll32/dsra32 $v0,$a1`). Every word from
+0x14 on is just the resulting shift (`s0` vs `s1` vs `s2`, ra offset,
epilogue), so the score sits at 43 despite the instruction stream matching
1:1 otherwise.

## Suggestion for Claude

The `daddu s0,a1,zero` before the 16-bit truncation looks like mwccps2
keeping the test operand in a callee-saved slot when the `bnez` and the
delay-slot `daddu s1,a0` are scheduled around a cold-placed if-body.
`tools/permute.py` has never been run on this family; a permuter run or a
scheduling insight (e.g. what C makes CW route the `(s16)` test through an
`s0` copy) would crack 0x104060, 0x104110 and likely the 0x1041C0 /
0x104440 twins too.
