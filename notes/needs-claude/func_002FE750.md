### Key findings (prior pass)

- The twelve groups **must** be twelve separate arms (one return-1 stub
  each). One `if` per run of constants gets range-folded by CW into
  `addiu v,-c ; sltiu $at,4 ; bnel` — the original never folds, even for
  0x62..0x65. (The switch form above escapes the fold without `u16`.)
- `u16 v1 = (u16)arg0;` kept CW from folding the runs in the if-chain form
  and pinned the subject to `$v0` / constants to `$v1` (an `s32` local goes
  the other way: `andi $v1,$a0` + `beq $v1,$v0`, like matched
  `func_002FE690`). The switch form keeps `$v0` with plain `s32`.
- `(u16)` + tail written as `return (v1==0x58) || (v1==0x5A)` or `?:` flips
  the register allocation back and folds nothing (factory-3 confirmed: the
  `||` form also moved the `andi` target to `$v1`).
- The prior pass concluded "switch lowers descending, own stub per case —
  no": wrong, that was ascending source order. Groups written DESCENDING
  give the ascending chain (reverse source order, idioms.md "Compare chain
  on constants"), one stub per group, and the last-tested single-instruction
  body fuses into the beql delay slot.
