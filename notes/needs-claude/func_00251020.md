- **What it does:** locks a semaphore (if id > 0), marks a buffer busy,
  splits an index into `(index / size) * size` at +0x2C and `index % size`
  at +0x30 (divisor reloaded from +0x0 between the two divides, both
  guarded by the div-guard), then releases the semaphore. `WaitSema` and
  `SignalSema` take the id via a delayed `lw $a0,0($a0)` on a pointer to

  `bgtz` and spilled in its delay slot to `0x3C($sp)`, and the second test
  and both calls read through the spilled pointer. The sibling function at
  0x251140 (unmatched, same .s file) repeats the exact
  `lw 0xC / addiu a0 / bgtz+sw-spill / out-of-line WaitSema` shape three
  times, so the idiom exists in this TU.
