3. The second table test has `andi $v1,$v1,0xFF` in its `beql` delay slot (mine: `nop`);
   mine hoists the byte-mask of `(n+0x11c)->byte` after the table.
4. The count loop (0x1F8–0x208) is a cold block at the tail with `b +0x2A<<2` back to
