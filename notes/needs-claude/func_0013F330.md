## Two blockers

1. **Base-register shuffle (+12 B).** The original keeps `daddu $s0,$a0` in
   the `beqz` delay slot and uses `s0 + immediate` for every field access.
   My compile instead emits `daddu $v1,$a0,$zero` at +0x04, keeps the 64-bit
   arg0 copy in `$v1`, reuses `$s0` as a scratch base (`addiu $s0,$v1,0xF4`,
   then `sw $v1,0x0($s0)` style accesses), and hoists four address
   materializations before their use: `addiu $a2,$v1,0xE8` (+0x20),
   `addiu $a0,$v1,0xF0` (+0x28), `addiu $a2,$v1,0xEC` (+0x4C),
   `addiu $s0,$v1,0xF4` (+0x74). Constant materialization also drifts
   (`addiu $a2,$zero,0x3F` lands late at +0x7C). Score is dominated by this
   one shift; the per-instruction shape is otherwise right (packet 1 store
   order 0,8,4,C matches once written in that source order).
2. **The 0xEC triplet.** Original: `sw $v0,0xEC($s0); lw $v0,0xEC($s0);
   sw $v0,0xEC($s0)` — identical encodings, a plain reload-and-restore.
   No plain C assignment produces it (`x = x` is lint-forbidden and contrived).
   Plausible sources: the member at 0xEC is written through two different
   types in the original struct (union of `char *` / `s32`), or CodeWarrior
   rematerialized it under register pressure — fixing (1) may make it appear
   on its own. Same triplet shape exists once elsewhere: `game_28A940.s`
   +0028A958 (different context, a loop bound there).
