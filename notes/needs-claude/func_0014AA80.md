## The three remaining diffs (all scheduler/layout, same blocks)
1. **Address CSE**: my store + reload of `arg0+0x14` share a materialized
   `addiu $a3/$t0,$a0,0x14` (hoisted into the beqz delay slot); the original uses
   direct `sw $v1,0x14($a0)` / `lw $v1,0x14($a0)` with a `nop` delay slot. Tried:
   `char *`/`char **` vs `s32` field types, a `(s32)`-cast reload, a nested
   `M2C_FIELD(M2C_FIELD(...))` — the addiu survived all spellings. The idioms
   "member setter fed by a member getter / Field load CSE'd where the original
   reloads" (`.cpp` + inline member functions) is the untried route for this one.
2. **bc1tl vs bc1t** on test 1: mine takes the likely form and hoists test 2's
   `lwc1 $f1,0x4($a2)` into its delay slot; the original is plain `bc1t` + `nop`
   and keeps both test-2 loads inside the test-2 block.
3. **Copy-block tail**: mine folds `sb $v1,0x29($a0)` into the `b` delay slot
   (5 words); the original keeps `sb` before the `b` with a `nop` delay slot

   `bc1t <return>; nop; b <copy+4>; lq` (copy is the fall-through), mine
