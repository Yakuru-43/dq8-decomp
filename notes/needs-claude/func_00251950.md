## The wall: register allocation (4 s regs vs 3 + one spill)

Target prologue: `addiu sp,-0x50; sd $ra,0x30($sp); sq $s2,0x20; sq $s1,0x10;
daddu $s2,$a0; sq $s0,0($sp); addiu $v1,$s2,0xC; lw $v0,0xC($a0); daddu $s1,$a1;
daddu $s0,$a2; bgtz $v0, cold; sw $v1,0x4C($sp); lw $v1,0x10($s2)`.

- The semaphore **pointer** `&obj->0xC` lives in $v1 from early, gets spilled to
  0x4C($sp) in the bgtz delay slot, and is reloaded only at the tail (`lw $v1,0x4C($sp)`,
  then `lw $v0,0($v1)` and the call arg `lw $a0,0($v1)`). Both later sema accesses go
  through the spilled pointer; only the first test load is folded to `lw $v0,0xC($a0)`
  (base $a0, not $s2!).
- Both my drafts pin sema in $s0 and obj in $s3 → 4 callee-saved, `sd $ra,0x40($sp)`,
  no stack slot. Everything downstream shifts: ret lands in $s1 loaded *after* the tail
  test; my wrap add is inlined (`bgtz $a0,+3; nop; lw $v0,8($s3); addu`) instead of the
  cold `blezl $a0,A14` + delayed `lw $v0,8($s2)`; my tail guard inverts to
  `lw $a0,0($s0); bgtz` → cold jal with `lw $s1,0x14($s3)` + `b epi; daddu $v0,$s1,$zero`.
  So the whole diff is one allocation decision, not logic.
