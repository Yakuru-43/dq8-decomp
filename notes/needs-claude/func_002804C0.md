## What it does
Struct copy `Unk88 *f(Unk88 *dst, Unk88 *src)` of a 0x88 struct, returns dst:
- 0x00–0x3C: 16 scalar words, unrolled `lw $v0/sw $v0` pairs (match exactly).
- 0x40–0x5C and 0x60–0x7C: two rolled loops, 4 iterations, 8-byte stride,
  2 words per iteration (`lw $v1,0(t0)`, `addiu a2,a2,-1`, `lw $v0,4(t0)`,
  `sw v1,0(a3)`, `addiu t0,t0,8`, `sw v0,4(a3)`, `bgtz a2`, delay `addiu a3,a3,8`).
  Setup `addiu t0,a1,0x40; addiu a3,a0,0x40; addiu a2,zero,4` is scheduled into
  the delay slots of the very first `lw 0(a1)`; loop 2's setup stays at the loop.
- 0x80, 0x84: two words via `$v1`, with `daddu $v0,$a0,$zero` (pointer-typed
  return) between them, store 0x84 in the `jr $ra` delay slot.
