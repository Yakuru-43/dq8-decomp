## Blocker

CodeWarrior **rematerializes the +0x118 base**: the original materializes
`entry = r + 0x118` as `addiu $s1,$v0,0x118` in the 359CF0 `jal` delay slot
(pre-null-check, speculated) and loads `lw $a1,0($s1)`; same for
`addiu $s4,$v0,0x118` + `lw $a1,0($s4)` after 2CD100. My C (`char *entry;
entry = r + 0x118; ... *(s32 *)entry`, and the inline `*(s32 *)(r + 0x118)`
form) makes CW recompute the address from the r register at the load
(`addiu $v1,$s2,0x118; lw $a1,0($v1)` or folded `lw $a1,0x118($s5)`), keeping
r itself in an s register instead of the entry pointer. Consequence: 6 saved
s-regs (frame 0x80, local at 0x70) instead of 5.

Also: original's `beqz $v0` tests the call result directly with `daddu $s4,$v0`
in the delay slot; mine tests the saved r (`beqz $s5`) with the copy emitted
before the branch.

MH2 ref consulted: Index lines 125/267-269 ("A load folded into its use",
"Address kept for a load and a later store", Addresses section 3005/3269) —
none of the listed fixes (typed view pointers, cast-at-use) reproduced the
delay-slot base materialization here.
