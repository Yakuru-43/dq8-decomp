## Why stuck
The original spills params 5,6,8 to the frame's top bytes `sb $tN,0xBF/BE/BD($sp)`
right in the prologue and reloads them with `lbu` + raw `bnez/beqz` (no andi);
mine (both `u8` and `bool` for those params) keeps all three in `$s` registers
(`daddu` + `andi 0xFF` copies) and instead spills `mobs`/`altId` to stack words
`sw $a0,0xCC($sp)`/`sw $a3,0xC8($sp)` — so the whole allocation shifts
(frame 0xD0 vs 0xC0, m in $s2 vs $s1, match in $s1 vs $s0, i in $s0) and every
later word is off. CLASS structural, score 118.

the callee `func_0029D170` is verified `s32 func_0029D170(void*)` — a plain
`s64` local gives `sd`, not `sq`.
