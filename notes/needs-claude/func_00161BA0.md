## What it does

Splits a path-ish buffer. `i = strlen(arg0) - 1`; scan `arg0[i]` downward to 0
for `/` (0x2F). Then:
- slash at i >= 1 (or not found, i == -1): copy `arg0[0..i]` (i+1 bytes, the
  `/` included; not-found copies 0 bytes) to arg1 with pointer bumps, then
  `strcpy(arg2, arg0 + i + 1)`, then `*dst = 0` (store lands in the jal delay
  slot).
- slash exactly at 0 (`beql $a1,$zero`, out-of-line block): no copy,
  `strcpy(arg2, arg0)`, `*arg1 = 0` — observably the same result as i == -1.
All three args stay in a0/a1/a2 through the strlen `jal` (pass-through).
Frame 0x40: `sd $ra,0x30` / `sq $s2,0x20` / `sq $s1,0x10` / `sq $s0,0`;
s2=arg0, s1=arg1 (the dst walked by the copy loop), s0=arg2.

## Diff (attempt 1)

One coherent register swap plus the 8 B tail:
- Original keeps the scan index `i` in `$a1` and the copy source pointer in
  `$a0` (mine had them the other way round): `addiu $a1,$v0,-1`, scan
  `addu $v1,$s2,$a1`/`bgez $a1`, loop test `slt $at,$a1,$v1`, load
  `lb $v0,0x0($a0)`, `daddu $a0,$s2` before the copy loop, `addiu $a0,$a0,1`.
- Original tail after the loop: `addiu $v0,$a1,1; daddu $a0,$s0; addu $a1,$s2,$v0;
  jal strcpy; sb $zero,0($s1)` then a shared exit stub `b <epi>; ld $ra,0x30($sp)`,
  and the cold `if (i == 0)` block `daddu $a1,$s2; jal strcpy; sb $zero,0($s1)`
  (delay) + `b <stub>; nop` back to that stub. Mine folded the trailing
  `*dst = 0;` into the delay slot of a `b` instead of the jal's, merged the
  stub, and came out 188 B (2 words short).

Everything else — scan loop (entry `b` to bottom `nop; bgez; nop`, `beq` to
found with nop delay, decrement after), `beql $a1,$zero` for `i == 0` with
`daddu $a0,$s0` in its delay slot, copy loop (`b`-in with `daddu $v1,$zero`
init, body `addiu $v1,1; sb $v0,0($s1); addiu src,1; addiu $s1,1`, test
`slt $at,i,n` + `beql` back with `lb $v0,0(src)` delay) — matched word for word.
