## The only remaining diff (loop back-edge scheduling)

Prologue, both exits and the calls match. The `lui/addiu` at +0x10 is the
D_004593D0 relocation, ignore. Mine vs original, +0x28..+0x4C:

+028  addu   $v0, $s1, $v0          +028  sll    $v0, $s0, 1
+02C  lh     $v0, 0x20($v0)         +02C  addu   $v0, $s1, $v0
+030  beq    $v0, $a0, found        +030  lh     $v0, 0x20($v0)
+034  nop                           +034  beq    $v0, $a0, found
+038  addiu  $s0, $s0, 0x1          +038  nop
+03C  slti   $v0, $s0, 0x4          +03C  addiu  $s0, $s0, 0x1
+040  bnel   $v0, $zero, body       +040  slti   $v0, $s0, 0x4
+044  sll    $v0, $s0, 1   <- hoisted  +044  bnez   $v0, body
+048  addiu  $s0, $zero, -0x1       +048  nop    <- empty slot

MWCC hoists the next iteration's `sll $v0,$s0,1` into the back-edge delay
slot and uses `bnel`; the game keeps it at the body top with a plain
`bnez` + nop (one word more). The whole function is that one word.
