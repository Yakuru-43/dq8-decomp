## The last word (+0xD4)
Everything else is byte-exact: prologue, zero loop, guard chain, the
`||`-shared clear block, counters, both calls, epilogue. Only:

    +0D4  mine  daddiu $a0, $a0,   1   (flag++ on u64)
    +0D4  orig  daddiu $a0, $zero, 1

Origins: `flag = 0` already emits `daddu $a0,$zero,$zero` (matches in every
spelling). The materialisation of 1 refuses `daddiu ..., $zero, 1`:

- `s32 flag; flag = 1;` → `addiu`
- `s64` / `u64 flag; flag = 1;` → `addiu` (same finding as the s64 draft in
  `func_002930A0.md` item 1 — same codebase, same chain, same bug)
- `flag = 1LL;`, `flag = (u64)1;` → `addiu`
- `u64 flag; flag++;` → `daddiu $a0,$a0,1` — right opcode, source register
  is `a0` not `zero`; the constant-zero (set by the `daddu` earlier in the
  block, across the `beqz` at +0C8) does not fold into the source.
