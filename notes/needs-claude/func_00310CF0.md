## The blocker — the double guard needs two registers
ROM: `jal 15F490 / daddu $s1,$v0 / beqz $s1 / daddu $s2,$v0 / beqz $s2 / nop`.
One getter, TWO saved locals, both copied from `$v0`, checked one after the
other, then split: s1 -> func_0016D5B0 arg0 (then reused as `entry`),
s2 -> func_001419A0 arg0. CodeWarrior will not keep two copies of one value.
This is the wall func_00310C60's note predicted for this function.

So: the cast direction is NOT the lever (A3's two copies come from the
collapse restructuring, not from the cast). CW's rules as observed:
u32<->Entry* casts are value-transparent (coalesce); a pointer non-NULL fact

fact (A1 checks survive). The wanted shape = A1's surviving checks WITH A3's
separate copies — i.e. two value-classes of equal value, neither provably
non-zero.
