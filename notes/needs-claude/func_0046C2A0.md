What it does: for each of 0x77 entries of the table at `*(u32)0x3E1848` (stride
0x30), if `e[0x14] > 0` scan `e[0x18 + j]` (j = 0..14, `lb`, signed bytes) for a
match with the `s32` global `D_003E014C` (0x3E014C); once found (sticky flag),
each iteration stores `e[0x28] = e[0x14] * (e[0x10] + 1)`,
`e[0x2C] = (e[0x10] * e[0x14]) / 240`, then +1 if `(e[0x10]*e[0x14]) % 240 > 0`.

## What was tried

- `(S_slot *)(D_003E1848 + i*0x30)` vs `(S_slot *)(i*0x30 + D_003E1848)` in the
  probe: identical codegen (CW normalizes the `+`), operand swap does not move
  the rs/rt choice. Also tried `*(s8 *)(D + i*0x30 + 0x18 + j)` (folded the 0x18
  into an `addiu` instead of the `lb` offset — the `bytes[j]` field form is what
  keeps `lb 0x18($t0)`).
- `bool` in `.c`: compile error (`bool` undefined); `.cpp` + `extern "C"` gives
  the `daddiu $a2,$zero,1` + raw `bnel` exactly (idioms.md "daddiu flag").
- Declaration order `bool found; s32 j;` (not `s32 j; bool found;`): required to
  put the counter in `$a1` and the flag in `$a2`.
- `tools/permute.py` finisher unusable on this host: `FileNotFoundError: 'cpp'`
  (same failure the twin `func_0045F270` note reports).
- mh2 catalogue entry tried: "Register clusters swapped around an or/addu"
  (operands in the other order) — that is the closest symptom; it did not move
  CW's choice here, so this may need the permuter or a struct-based address
  spelling (e.g. a second struct view with the byte field at 0x18 named, or
  `e->bytes` used through a re-derived pointer).
