# func_00141830 (128 B incl. 8 B padding, 0x141830..0x1418B0)

Twin of `func_00141750` (see `func_00141750.md`, same session), going the
other direction: copies four `s32` from `u32 *src` (arg2) into stack `q[4]`
at `0x10..0x1C`, divides each by 16 with **`sra`** (signed `>> 4`), and
calls `func_001414D0(d, a, q, b, ...)` with `a2 = sp+0x10`.
It also **re-reads and modifies `$t0` and `$t1`** at entry (`sra $t0,$t0,4`
in the first delay slot, `sra $t1,$t1,4` at +0x1C) — those are its own
`param_reg(8)/(9)` arguments, divided by 16 and forwarded to
`func_001414D0`'s `param_reg` args; `$t2` passes through untouched.
Entry registers match `func_00141750`'s exit (it is the caller chain:
`func_00141750 -> func_00141020 -> ... -> func_00141830 -> func_001414D0`,
see also `func_001414D0.md` for the `$t0/$t1/$t2` story).

## Best attempt: score 12, 120 vs 120 bytes
`notes/needs-claude/func_00141830_best.c` — two-phase unrolled C (four raw
copies, then four `>>= 4`, scale order q0,q1,q3,q2), call at the end.
Prologue through +0x2C matches exactly (including the `sra $t1` at +0x1C
and the three slot-pointer addiu's in `$t6/$t5/$t4` — do **not** write
`src[i] >> 4` directly: single-phase fuses and comes out 84 vs 120, and the
raw store + reload per element is essential).
The last 12 words are a one-deep register rotation: the original scales
q0→`$v1` (stored in the `jal` delay slot at +0x68), q1→`$t3` at +0x44,
q3→`$a2` (reused arg reg) at +0x4C, q2→`$v0` at +0x58; mine holds them in
`$t3/$v0/$v1` shifted by one stage. The scale-order q0,q1,q2,q3 gives the
same score 12.
