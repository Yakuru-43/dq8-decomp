## The wall (only diff, +0x50..tail)

ROM:
```
+04C dsll32 $v1,$a1,24      +068 sltu  $v0,$a2,$a0
+050 dsra32 $v1,$v1,24      +06C bnez  $v0, +0x54   (back edge)
+054 lb     $v0,0($a2)      +070 nop
+058 beql  $v0,$v1, +0x68   +074 nop    <-- extra junction block
+05C  addiu $a2,+1          (delay)     +078 addiu $v0,$zero,-1
+060 b      +0x7C                       +07C jr $ra
+064  subu  $v0,$a2,$a3     (delay)     +080 nop (pad)
```
So the ROM needs BOTH: (a) entry falling through `dsra32` into the loop top
(beql-equal->bottom with `p++` in the delay slot, separate `b` to the
found-return), AND (b) an empty block after the loop exit, before the shared
`-1` materialization, entered only by the exhaustion fall-through. The do-while
gets (a) but merges the junction into the stub (128 B).
