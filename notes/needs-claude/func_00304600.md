## What worked (maybe an idiom for the skill)
`slti $v0` + non-likely `bnez` with the delay slot filled by a constant store
(0x46D4/0x8C8-style blocks) does **not** come from if/else (that gives
`beql`+`$at` inverted, or `bnel`): it comes from a default assignment then a
conditional overwrite, e.g. `ret = 0xE; if (index >= 2) { ret = 0xF; }` — the
unconditional store is hoisted into the branch delay slot and dies on the
not-taken path. All six `< 2` blocks went from wrong to byte-exact with this
form (score 52 → 28).
