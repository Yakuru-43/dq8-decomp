## What is wrong vs ROM

Everything after the prologue is a **one-off schedule/register cascade caused by
the memset not being inlined** (mine emits
`addiu a0,sp,0x4B8 / daddu a1,zero,zero / jal memset / addiu a2,zero,6`; the ROM
has the inline guard+loop). The `jal memset` clobbers $a1-$a3, so CW copies
arg1-3 to $s2/$s1/$s0 in the prologue and schedules the three `sh` stores AFTER
the `func_00222400`... er, after the `func_002E1F40` arg setup — the ROM stores
them from the arg registers before `ori $a1,$zero,0xFD1B`. Once memset inlines,
the s-register allocation should also follow (ROM s6=arg6,s5=arg0,s4=arg4,
s3=arg5,s2=i,s1=dest,s0=j,s7=&sel[i]; mine had s7=arg6 + the copies squatting
s2/s1/s0).
