## Walls
1. **Register allocation / frame**: needs the original's exact local lifetime
   order (declaration order, per the P18 idiom, at ~25 locals incl. five
   11-word digit tables and six buffers). My locals are semantically right
   but allocated differently, so all argument loads use different `s` regs.
2. **Scale load**: original is one `lwc1 $f20,0x184(f124)`; my `struct Quad`
   read emitted `lq 0x180 / sq 0x640 / lwc1 0x644` (the two-u32 form). A
   plain `f32` field read at +0x184 should give the single lwc1.
3. **Delay-slot theft**: digit loop heads are `slt v0,s5,s3; bnel body /
   addu v0,s5,sp` and the digit-10 clamp is an out-of-line `b back;
   addiu v0,10` (P14/P15/P25/P37 family).
4. **Dead pair before `func_1814D0`**: footer loop has `sll v0,s3,1;
   addu v0,s1,v0;` immediately before `jal func_1814D0`, result never used
   (func_1814D0 is the matched no-arg getter, see src/SLES_539.74/func_001814D0.c).
   No legal C produces instructions feeding a no-arg call; likely the original
   callee took an arg (or it is scheduler residue). This is where a real C++
   member call with a `this`-like arg may be hiding.
5. **Stale args**: `func_1FFC30` row passes a stale a2 to `1339E0` (I guessed
   buf340); `func_34E590` is called with stale a3 (arr230 address) — P16's
   note says its three callees take 7/5/4 args.
6. `D_003E0098` is main-binary gp-relative (assembler warns MENU gp_value
   0x3E7270); it linked fine **without** adding `_gp` — no config change made.
