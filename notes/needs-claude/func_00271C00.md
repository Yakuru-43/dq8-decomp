`tag` in the high u16. Returns `rec->items + index*16` (items at +0x08, 16-byte
records) when `0 < key`, `index < rec->count` (+0x04), and `rec->tags[index]`
(+0x14, u32*) equals `tag`; else NULL. Neighbours 271B40..271BC0 are float
accessor stubs (same TU).
