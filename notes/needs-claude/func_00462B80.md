## The one diff

Original, `func_1419A0` call region (mine in brackets):

```
    jal        func_00462CE0
     daddu     $s0, $v0, $zero     [mine: nothing here — result kept in $v0]
    lui        $v1, (0x3F800000 >> 16)
    daddu      $a0, $s0, $zero     [mine: daddu $a0, $v0, $zero]
    mtc1       $v1, $f12
    jal        func_1419A0
     daddu     $a1, $v0, $zero     [mine: same]
```

CW copies `render` into a saved `$s0` in the producer's delay slot and takes `$a0`
from `$s0`; my C (`render = func_00462CE0(...); func_1419A0(render, render, 1.0f);`)
keeps it in `$v0` and is one word short (344 vs 348). Everything before +0x5C and after
+0x90 is word-identical (gp_rel relocations `0x0($gp)` and the shifted branch targets
are the only `*` there). `$s0` is free again at +0x88 (`daddu $s0,$zero` = `i = 0`), so
`render`'s live range ends at the `func_1419A0` call in any spelling I found.
