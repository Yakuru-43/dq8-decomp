## What is wrong vs ROM (best attempt)

1. **s-register allocation inverted.** ROM: ctr→s0, mode→s1, n→s2 (prologue and
   arg copies s3/s4/s5 byte-exact, `sq $s0` in the first jal delay slot matches).
   Mine: mode→s0, n→s1, ctr→s2 — tried declaration orders {table,ctr,mode,n},

   the source shape makes CW prefer ctr first; the P18 "declaration order" lever
   did not move it (possibly because of the `f32 table[4]` array or the float
   punning).
2. **`addiu $s1,$v0,-1` timing.** ROM has it the slot after the 21B220 delay slot;
   mine hoists one `lwc1` above it (v0 stays live, no call between).
3. **Table init schedule.** ROM interleaves `lui v0,hi / lwc1 f / swc1 f,0x?0(sp)`
   (store right after its load) and hoists `lui a0,%hi(D_003FAFB0)` +
   `addiu a0,%lo` to before the block; mine batches all four lwc1, then all four
   swc1, and puts the a0 pair late; `sll/addu` land after the third swc1 in ROM,
   mid-block in mine.
4. **func_00176230 arg.** ROM: `lb v0,D_003E1190; sltu v0,zero,v0; xori v0,1;` then
