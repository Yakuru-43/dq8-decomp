## The blocker

The original keeps the `func_00460D70` result in a callee-saved register:
prologue `sq $s1,0x10($sp)` / `daddu $s1,$a0` (arg0→s1) / `sq $s0,0($sp)`,
frame 0x30, `sd $ra,0x20($sp)`; after the call `daddu $s0,$v0,$zero`, and both
arms feed `daddu $a0,$s0,$zero` (three redundant copies, one in the `b` delay
slot, one after the else-arm `jal`). My byte-identical-shape C keeps `temp_v0`
in `$v0` (frame 0x20, only arg0 in `$s0`, 216 B): the compare `bnel` tests
`$v0` vs `$v1` and the arms copy `daddu $a0,$v0,$zero`. Everything shifts
after +0x0 and the epilogue loses the `lq $s1`.

`daddu $s0,$v0,$zero` is emitted *immediately after the jal* (delay slot is
`nop`), before `addiu $v0,$zero,0x4` — so CW home'd the value into `$s0` at
its def, not at first use. `$s1`/`$s0` also match the declaration-order idiom
(arg0→higher reg) *if* the temp is callee-saved at all.
