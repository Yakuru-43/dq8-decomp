## Two things the original does that my C does not

1. **Base materialization.** The original emits `addiu $a0,$a0,0x2D0` early
   (right after the guards, before the bit block) and then
   `addu $v0,$a0,$v0; lw $v0,0x0($v0)`. Any single-use pointer I write
   (`(s32 *)((char *)arg0 + 0x2D0)` inside the else, or `arg0 = arg0 + 0xB4`
   on an `s32 *`) gets its constant folded into the load: CW emits
   `addu $v0,$v0,$a0; lw $v0,0x2D0($v0)`. Assigning `bitmap` *before* the
   guards prevents the fold but then CW hoists the `addiu` into the first
   branch's delay slot (into `$v1`, not `$a0`) and swaps the word/bit block
   order.
2. **Tail shape.** Original: main path `and; b .LDF0; sltu` (the sltu sits in
   the unconditional jump's delay slot), then the shared zero block
   `.LDEC: daddu $v0,$zero,$zero` falls through to `jr`. Best attempt produced
   `and; sltu; b; nop; b; daddu; jr` — one instruction too long (108 vs 104),
   sltu not in a delay slot.

Also note the original's straight-line order: base, `li $v1,1`, bit block
(`% 32` + `sllv`), **then** word block (`/ 32` + `sll`) — the opposite of the
sibling `func_00307E00`, so CW does follow source order here.
